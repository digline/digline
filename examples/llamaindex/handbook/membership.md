# The annual pass

An annual pass costs ninety pounds and covers unlimited hires on the same terms
as a single one. It runs for twelve months from the day it is bought and does
not renew by itself.

A pass may be cancelled within fourteen days of purchase for a full refund,
provided no hire has been started on it. After a hire, the refund is the unused
whole months, calculated from the day the cancellation is received.
