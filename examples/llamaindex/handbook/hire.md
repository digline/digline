# Hiring a bike

A hire begins when the bike leaves the dock and ends when it locks into
another one. The first thirty minutes of every hire are included in the pass;
after that the meter runs at one pound per additional half hour, rounded up.

A hire that passes twenty-four hours is closed automatically and the bike is
recorded as missing. The charge for a missing bike is one hundred and fifty
pounds, and it is refunded in full if the bike is returned to any dock.
