# When the dock is full

If every point at a dock is taken, use the screen on the dock post to claim
fifteen extra minutes at no charge. The claim is per ride, not per day, and it
begins the moment you press it rather than when you arrive.

The screen also lists the three nearest docks with a free point. Those counts
are live and refresh every ten seconds, so a dock shown as free may fill while
you are riding to it.
