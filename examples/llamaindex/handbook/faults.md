# A fault during a ride

Report a brake, tyre or gear fault from the ride screen before you finish, and
the meter stops at the moment you report it. A fault reported after the bike is
docked does not stop the meter, because the ride is already closed.

Never leave a faulty bike outside a dock. Push it to the nearest dock, lock it,
and press the red button on the dock post: that marks the bike out of service
so nobody hires it after you.
